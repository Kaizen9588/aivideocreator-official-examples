# Prompt recipes (published subset)

These are the prompts behind the official example clips, plus more starting points for your own scenes.

> **This is a published subset.** The full style library and the prompt-engineering layer live inside the product (the **Enhance** button expands any one-liner into a timed 0–15s shot list). What is published here is enough to judge the output and start making your own clips.

## The formula

One to three vivid sentences covering five ingredients:

**Subject → Action → Setting → Camera → Mood & sound**

| Ingredient | Question it answers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Who or what is in frame? | a paper boat |
| Action | What happens? | drifts past windows |
| Setting | Where and when? | a glowing night city |
| Camera | How does the lens move? | low angle, slow drift |
| Mood & sound | How should it feel? | calm, quiet synth hum |

Leave out: real people, brands, logos, model names, quality incantations, conflicting camera moves, length instructions (clips are fixed at 15 seconds).

## Official clips

### Stadium goal at night — `videos/stadium-goal.mp4`

```text
A striker in a red kit beats the last defender and finishes under
floodlights, low camera at pitch level, crowd roar.
```

Why it works: one subject, one decisive action, a place everyone can picture, a camera that puts you on the grass, and a soundtrack cue ("crowd roar") that gives the generated audio something to build.

### Lantern festival by the river — `videos/lantern-festival.mp4`

```text
A woman releases a paper lantern onto a dark river, hundreds of
lights rising around her, slow push-in, soft wind and bells.
```

Why it works: the release is a single gesture with a natural payoff; "hundreds of lights rising" gives the 15 seconds a shape (build → release); the audio cue is quiet and specific.

### Paper boat in a glowing city — `videos/paper-boat-vertical.mp4`

```text
A paper boat drifts through a glowing night city, low angle close
to the water, quiet synth hum.
```

Why it works: a small subject in a big world is instantly readable in 9:16; the low angle makes the vertical frame earn its keep.

## Starting points (not shown as clips — your first drafts)

```text
A night market on Mars, handheld camera, warm lanterns, a small
rover weaving through the crowd.
```

```text
Sizzling noodles tossed in a wok by a street cook, steam catching
neon light, slow-motion close-up, sizzle and chatter.
```

```text
A corgi bounding through autumn leaves toward the camera, low
tracking shot, golden afternoon, playful and bright.
```

```text
A train crossing a stone viaduct over a foggy valley, drone
pull-back, early morning haze, distant rumble.
```

```text
Rain on a café window at night, warm interior glow, bokeh
streetlights, static frame with a gentle focus pull, muffled jazz.
```

```text
A runner's shoes hitting wet asphalt at dawn, extreme close-up,
rhythmic breathing and steady footsteps.
```

```text
A sneaker rotating on a matte pedestal, studio softbox light, slow
orbit, clean minimal hum.
```
