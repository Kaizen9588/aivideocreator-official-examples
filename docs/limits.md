# Honest limits

What AI Video Creator does not do (yet) — stated plainly, because every tool has limits and pretending otherwise wastes your time.

## Format limits

- **Length is fixed at 15 seconds.** There is no 30-second or 60-second mode. The format was chosen deliberately — it is where short-form watch-through is strongest — but if you need a long-form piece, this is not the tool.
- **The frame is always 9:16 vertical.** No landscape or square output.
- **The free preview is 768p and 5 seconds.** Judge it as a direction check; the paid format is the 15-second 2K clip.

## Creative limits

- **No timeline editing.** If you want a different result, you change the prompt and regenerate. There is no drag-and-drop, no layer panel, no manual cuts.
- **Real people, brands, and logos are not supported.** Named celebrities, trademarked characters, and logos will not be reproduced. Describe the *look* you want instead — "a striker in a red kit", not a player's name.
- **Audio is scene sound and music, not scripted voiceover.** Every clip ships with generated ambience and music matched to the scene. If you need narration, the MP4 downloads clean and takes a voiceover layer in any editor.
- **One camera idea per clip.** Prompts with two or three competing camera moves render as mush.

## Operational limits

- **Generation takes minutes, not seconds.** Budget a few minutes per clip; you can close the tab and collect the result later.
- **Runs vary.** The same prompt twice gives related but not identical clips. If the execution is off but the idea is right, regenerate before rewriting the prompt.

## Pricing facts (no surprises)

- One free preview per email-verified account (768p, 5 seconds, sound included).
- Packs are one-time purchases and never expire: $9.9 / 2 videos, $19.9 / 5, $39.9 / 11.
- If a generation fails, the credit is returned to your balance automatically — failed runs never cost a video.
