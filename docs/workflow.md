# From one sentence to a posted TikTok or Reel

The complete workflow inside AI Video Creator (aivideocreator.io). No editing app involved at any step.

## 0. Account

Sign up with an email code or Google. Verifying your email unlocks the **free preview**: one 768p, 5-second clip with sound, so you can judge the direction before paying.

## 1. Write the scene

One sentence: subject, action, setting, camera, mood. Recipes live in [`examples/prompts.md`](../examples/prompts.md). Do not write a script or a shot list — one vivid sentence outperforms a paragraph of instructions.

## 2. Press Enhance

Enhance expands your one-liner into a **timed 0–15 second shot list**: what the camera does at each beat, what changes on screen, and what the soundscape and music are doing. Your idea is preserved; the pacing becomes deliberate. Read what it wrote — if it invented something you did not mean, edit your original sentence and re-run Enhance.

## 3. Generate

Most clips reach the result page within a few minutes; you can close the tab and pick the video up later from **My videos**. The finished format is always the same: 15 seconds, 2K, 9:16 vertical, native stereo sound, single MP4.

## 4. Get it onto your phone

Two ways from the result page:

- **Download the MP4** to your computer, or
- **Scan the QR code** shown on the result page — it opens a save page on your phone and the video lands in your camera roll.

The QR route is usually faster, because your phone is where TikTok and Instagram live anyway.

## 5. Post it

- **TikTok**: tap **+**, upload from camera roll, caption and hashtags, post. The clip needs no cutting, muting, or resizing — it already plays in TikTok's native 9:16 frame with sound.
- **Instagram Reels**: upload, pick a cover frame with one clear subject, write a first-line caption that opens a loop, add two or three specific hashtags. Covers and captions are added in Instagram, not in the video.
